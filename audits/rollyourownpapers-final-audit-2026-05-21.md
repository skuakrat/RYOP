## 2. Why It Dropped — Three Phases

### Phase 1 — The blog explosion (mid-November 2025)

Between Nov 10 and Nov 24, RYOP went from **2 keywords in positions 1–3** to **19** — a
10× increase in two weeks. Ahrefs-estimated traffic jumped from 232 to 2,095 in a single
week. This was a mass blog-publishing event: dozens of informational posts about rolling
paper alternatives, weed culture, and consumer how-to topics were published simultaneously
and began ranking almost immediately.

These posts ranked fast because the queries were low-competition and the content was
topically matched — but they lacked genuine E-E-A-T signals (no named authors, no
first-hand expertise, AI-content patterns). Google granted temporary visibility while
collecting quality signals.

### Phase 2 — December 2025 Core Update (Dec 11–29) → effects Jan–Mar 2026

**What it targeted:** Anonymous content, low-effort AI-written material, sites without
identifiable author expertise. The update specifically penalised blogs where no human
expert was attributed.

**RYOP's exposure:** Every blog post lacks author attribution. The content pattern
(identical structure, emoji titles, "Ultimate Guide" naming, forward-dated slugs) matches
AI-content classifiers. 684 indexed blog posts with no named author is exactly what this
update targeted.

**Effect in data:** GSC clicks declined ~35% from Jan to Mar (1,000 → 580/day). Positions
stayed similar (5.0–5.5 average) but CTR fell — indicating Google may have begun
suppressing these posts in rich features before fully demoting their positions.

### Phase 3 — March 2026 Core Update (Mar 27 – Apr 8) → effects April–May 2026

**What it targeted:** Holistic Core Web Vitals scoring (site-wide, not per-page) and
continued E-E-A-T enforcement. Sites where the majority of content lacked genuine
expertise signals were re-evaluated.

**Effect in data:**
- GSC average position jumped from ~5.0 to **7.2** in the week of April 20 (two weeks
  after the update completed — typical lag time).
- Ahrefs Pos 1–3 keywords declined from 29 to 20 (then continued falling to 9 by May 18).
- Most significantly: **AI Overviews owned collapsed from 15 → 0** in the week of May 11.

### The AI Overview collapse — the most alarming signal

RYOP had been featured in **12–15 Google AI Overviews** through Feb–Apr 2026. By May 11
this dropped to **zero** — all AI Overview citations were lost simultaneously. This is
not a coincidence: Google's AI Overviews apply the same quality standards as the core
ranking algorithm. The same content-quality failures that caused positions to drop also
caused AI Overview eligibility to be revoked.

The collapse of 15 AI Overview features also explains why the May GSC click drop is
*larger* than the position movement would suggest: positions dropped somewhat, but RYOP
also stopped appearing in AI features that were generating additional clicks above the
organic results.

## 4. Confirmed Technical Issues (from theme export)

### 🔴 The `| within: collection` URL-splitting bug — CONFIRMED, 8 instances

Every product link from a collection page generates a non-canonical URL
(`/collections/X/products/Y`) instead of the canonical `/products/Y`. This splits
PageRank across two URL versions for every product.

**Files to fix:**

| File | Lines |
|------|-------|
| `snippets/product-grid-item.liquid` | 82, 337 |
| `snippets/product-grid-item-video.liquid` | 82, 340 |
| `sections/featured-collection.liquid` | 68 |
| `snippets/product-template.liquid` | 66, 662 |
| `snippets/onboarding-product-grid-item.liquid` | 3 |

**Change:** `{{ product.url | within: collection }}` → `{{ product.url }}`
and `{{ variant.url | within: collection }}` → `{{ variant.url }}`

### 🟠 No author attribution on any blog post

The theme's `article-template.liquid` section renders blog posts without named authors.
The December 2025 update specifically penalised this. Every post needs a real name, a
2–3 sentence bio with manufacturing credentials, and a `BlogPosting` schema block with
`author` entity.

### 🟠 Homepage H1 is the logo

`<h1 class="site-header__logo">Roll Your Own Papers</h1>` — the primary H1 is the logo
wrapper. A second H1 exists in the AI-generated hero. Fix: one descriptive H1 per page,
keyword-bearing, in the hero section.

### 🟡 WebSite schema is bare

`WebSite` JSON-LD exists but has no `potentialAction` / `SearchAction`. `Organization`
schema is missing (only legacy `http://` microdata on the logo). Add a full `Organization`
block with founding date, TÜV SÜD certification, and `sameAs` links.

### 🟡 AI crawler access (robots.txt / llms.txt)

Still returning 403 to this audit environment. Real Googlebot is unaffected (GSC proves
it). However, AI crawlers (OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot) may be blocked — given the AI
Overview collapse, confirm these crawlers are whitelisted. Add `/llms.txt`.

## 5. Fix Plan — Prioritised by Impact

### 🔴 Critical — Do this week (addresses the active crash)

**1. Add author attribution to every blog post — today.**
The December Core Update loss and the continued decline are directly caused by this gap.
Pick one real person (manufacturing director, founder, or lead designer). Write a
150-word bio with verifiable credentials. Add to all 684+ posts via a Shopify theme
author block and `BlogPosting` + `author` JSON-LD schema. This is the single highest-
leverage action.

**2. Audit and improve the 20 highest-traffic blog posts.**
Your top 20 posts drive ~70% of blog clicks. These must have:
- Named author with bio
- Unique, first-hand insight (not generic how-to)
- At least one internal link to a relevant commercial page
- Clean `<title>` (no emojis in the `<title>` tag)

Do not rewrite everything — prioritise the top 20.

**3. Recover AI Overviews.**
You had 15 AI Overview features and lost all of them. The path back:
- Fix author attribution (above) — this is the primary reason they were lost
- Ensure OAI-SearchBot is not blocked in robots.txt (verify in browser, not via this
  audit tool which is blocked by WAF)
- Add `/llms.txt` to give AI systems a structured overview of the site
- Write passage-length (130–160 word) self-contained answer blocks in blog content

### 🟠 High — Within 2 weeks

**4. Build out `/collections/custom-rolling-papers`.**
This page needs to rank for "custom rolling papers" (currently pos 18.5). Add 600–800
words of genuine manufacturer content: material comparison (hemp/rice/unbleached from
first-hand production experience), the customisation process step by step, MOQ
explanation, certification proof (TÜV SÜD), and who it's for (dispensaries, cannabis
brands, event planners). This content must sound like a manufacturer wrote it, not a
copywriter.

**5. Fix the `| within: collection` bug** (8 lines listed above).
Consolidates product link equity to canonical URLs. Takes 30 minutes.

**6. Link every relevant blog post to commercial pages.**
Your 684 blog posts get 120,000 clicks per 6 months. Currently almost none of that
traffic flows to commercial pages. Add a contextual paragraph + CTA in each relevant
post linking to `/collections/custom-rolling-papers` with descriptive anchor text like
"custom branded rolling papers for dispensaries".

### 🟡 Medium — Within 4 weeks

**7. Stop publishing more consumer/DIY blog posts.**
Every additional "alternatives to rolling paper" or "how to roll" post deepens the
topical mismatch that caused this drop. Freeze the current content strategy.

**8. Consolidate duplicate blog clusters.**
You have at least two near-duplicate clusters:
- "where to buy rolling paper" (3+ posts) → merge into one, 301 the rest
- "how to roll / use rolling paper" (4+ posts) → merge into one, 301 the rest

**9. Add `Organization` + `BreadcrumbList` schema.**
Add a proper `Organization` JSON-LD block to the homepage (founding date 2011, TÜV SÜD
credential, sameAs Trustpilot + Instagram). Add `BreadcrumbList` to collection and
product pages.

**10. Core Web Vitals.**
Run PageSpeed Insights mobile on `rollyourownpapers.com` and
`/collections/custom-rolling-papers`. The holistic CWV scoring from March 2026 means
one slow page affects all rankings. Fix: preload hero image with `fetchpriority="high"`,
add explicit `width`/`height` to all product images, defer the 3D visualizer JS.
