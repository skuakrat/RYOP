How We Manufacture Your Custom Rolling Papers

Every order follows the same quality-controlled 7-step process:

  1. Paper sourcing — We use organic hemp, ultra-thin rice, and natural unbleached wood pulp. Paper stock is selected to your specification.
  2. Artwork preparation — Our in-house design team prepares your print-ready artwork (free of charge). We check bleed, colour profiles, and text legibility before a single sheet is printed.
  3. Edge-to-edge full-colour printing — Your logo and design are printed across the entire booklet cover using premium food-grade inks. Optional: foiling, spot UV, embossing.
  4. Cutting and perforation — Sheets are precision-cut and perforated to your chosen size (1¼, King Size Slim, King Size Wide, or custom).
  5. Booklet assembly — Papers are assembled into booklets (standard 32 leaves or custom count) with optional magnetic closure, built-in crutch/filter tips, and custom inner printing.
  6. Quality control inspection — Each batch undergoes a multi-point quality check: print alignment, gum strip consistency, paper weight, and booklet closure. Defective units are removed before packing.
  7. Packaging and dispatch — Orders ship worldwide with tracking. Standard lead time from proof approval: 2.5 weeks.
