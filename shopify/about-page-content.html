About Roll Your Own Papers — custom rolling paper manufacturer since 2011

Roll Your Own Papers makes custom rolling papers, pre-roll cones, accessories, and packaging for brands and dispensaries. We started as a print shop in 2011 and have built out from there.

Our journey

We started as a printing house. Early on, customers in the Roll Your Own (RYO) and Make Your Own (MYO) market kept asking for more — UV printing on accessories, custom trays, cones, packaging. We added things as the demand came in, not all at once.

Where we are today

We're based in China, which keeps costs down and lead times predictable for buyers in North America, Europe, and Australia. Most of what we do is repeat business. That suits the way we work — it's easier to get details right with customers who already know what they want.

Our product range includes:

  • Custom Rolling Papers
  • Custom Pre-Roll Cones
  • Custom Rolling Trays
  • Custom Tips & Pre-Roll Tips / Accessories
  • Custom Folding Cartons & Flexible Packaging
  • Custom Swag for Retailers

Production capabilities

Our current factory setup:

  • 3 fully automated booklet packaging lines
  • 2 automated pre-roll cone lines
  • 2 3D UV printing lines
  • 2 plastic injection lines
  • 1 glass blowing line
  • 5 labor assembly lines
  • Advanced lithographic and flexographic presses for folding cartons and flexible packaging
  • Dedicated box forming production line

The same lines handle small branded runs and large-volume orders. QC doesn't vary by order size.

Why work with us

We've been making these products since 2011. We know this market, we know what buyers actually need, and we know what breaks down when communication isn't clear early. Most of our customers have been ordering for years. If that's the kind of working relationship you're after, we're easy to reach.
